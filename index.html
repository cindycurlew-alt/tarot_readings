Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

What can I expect from a reading? +
Each reading is a sacred space where we explore your questions through the wisdom of the tarot. You'll receive honest, compassionate insights that illuminate your path and empower your decisions. Readings are conducted via video call for a personal, intimate experience.
Do I need to prepare anything? +
Come with an open heart and clear intentions. It helps to have specific questions in mind, though open-ended readings are equally powerful. Find a quiet space where you won't be disturbed, and allow yourself to be fully present.
How often should I get a reading? +
This is deeply personal. Some clients return monthly for guidance, while others come during major life transitions. Trust your intuition—you'll know when it's time to reconnect with the cards.
Is everything confidential? +
Absolutely. Your reading is a sacred, private experience. Everything shared during our session remains completely confidential. Your trust is honored and protected.
